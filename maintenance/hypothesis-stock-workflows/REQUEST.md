# Hypothesis stock workflow survey scope

The user requests investigation of at least ten agent workflows for accumulating
novel research hypotheses and an improvement proposal for this repository.
This task produces survey/proposal documents only. Do not implement workflow
changes, modify research records, replenish the actual queue, run experiments or
call Qwen or another trial model. Existing dirty work and prior evidence remain.

Research scope: publicly accessible primary titles, abstracts, official project
pages and repository READMEs about scientific hypothesis generation, literature
grounding, novelty checks, memory, critique, diversity and experimental selection.
Search and inspect at least twelve distinct workflow families, targeting six in
each research slice. Count a paper and its code, or versions of one system, once.
No full-paper downloads/OCR or user-managed reference payloads. Do not treat agent
consensus, novelty scores, missing search hits or author benchmarks as proof that
a hypothesis is undiscovered. Distinguish sourced mechanism, reported evaluation,
known limitation, and the proposed transfer to this repository.

Required record per workflow: name, date/version as verified, primary URL,
generation/search mechanism, novelty/dedup mechanism, memory/stock mechanism,
validation strength and limitation, useful proposed adaptation. Absence of a
described feature must be marked undocumented in the inspected source, not absent
from the system. Prefer 60-100 words per workflow and avoid long quotations.

The supervisor integrates evidence and proposes a minimal state/data/gating design
with acceptance tests, priorities and tradeoffs. One independent read-only plan
audit follows; no review panel, repeat votes or replacement auditor. Research
agents have disjoint source families; a repository explorer independently maps
existing contracts. Save each compact report. Models for planning/review are
gpt-6-astra/high, fresh context. Gate must return delegate before each dispatch.

Repository review reads only exact relevant skill/prompt/record-contract files,
not live idea contents, completed experiments or raw reference/data payloads.
Initial budget: 900 lines of relevant docs; at most 500 lines of directly relevant
validator/router clauses if an enforced-contract assertion requires them. The
explorer reports actual behavior separately from recommendations.

English outside human/. Deliver a primary-source survey and implementation-ready
proposal in maintenance/hypothesis-stock-workflows, plus a Japanese explanation in
human/. Established repository formats/destinations govern this local document
task; no external Page, message, new task or automation is requested.
